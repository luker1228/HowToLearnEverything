# Do You Like Sports?

## Why this topic

Sports is a classic small-talk trap for people like me.

Someone asks *Do you like sports?* My brain runs a boolean check, returns `true` or `false`, and the conversation dies. I treat the question like a form field. But in real life, nobody wants a checkbox — they want a tiny window into your life: what you do with your body when work lets go, how you feel after moving, who you do it with.

I'm not a "sports person" in the gym-bro sense. I care more about **feeling less stuck in my head**. That soft truth is already more chatty than "Yes, I like sports."

---

## The thinking chain for this topic

| Step | My fill-in |
|---|---|
| ① Feel first | After I move, I feel lighter — brain noise goes down. |
| ② Scene | Weekend badminton with friends / a short walk after dinner. |
| ③ One detail | The sound of the shuttle, or streetlights on the walk home. |
| ④ Soft stance | Kind of… not hardcore. More "I need to move" than "I love competition." |
| ⑤ Door | You more into team stuff, or solo movement? |

---

## Dialogue (QA × 6 turns)

### Q1 — entry question

**Q:** Do you like sports?

**A (programmer default):**  
> Yes. / No.

**A (sensory chat):**  
> Kind of… not in a hardcore way. I don't really watch matches or keep scores. But when I move — even a bit — I feel lighter, like my brain finally shut up. What about you?

**Chain used:** soft stance → feel → door  
**Why it works:** "Kind of" refuses the boolean. One feeling word invites follow-up.

---

### Q2 — what kind?

**Q:** What kind of sports do you do?

**A (programmer default):**  
> Badminton. Sometimes running.

**A (sensory chat):**  
> Mostly badminton with friends on weekends — nothing serious, just sweating and laughing when someone hits a terrible shot. And if I'm alone, a short walk after dinner is enough. I'm not training for anything. I just don't want to live only in my chair.

**Chain used:** scene → detail (laughing / terrible shot) → soft stance  
**Steal this:** *"I'm not training for anything"* = soft, human, anti-resume energy.

---

### Q3 — how often / lifestyle

**Q:** How often do you exercise?

**A (programmer default):**  
> Two or three times a week.

**A (sensory chat):**  
> Honestly? It depends on my week. When work is heavy, I go quiet and sit too much — then my shoulders feel like stone. Lately I've been trying to protect at least one "move day," even if it's just a long walk. Not perfect, but better than nothing.

**Chain used:** soft stance (depends) → body feeling → recent habit  
**Steal this:** body metaphor — *shoulders feel like stone*.

---

### Q4 — feeling after

**Q:** How do you feel after you play?

**A (programmer default):**  
> Good. Tired but good.

**A (sensory chat):**  
> Tired in a clean way. You know that feeling when your head has been open all day — tabs everywhere — and after you move, half of them finally close? That's me. Also hungry. Always hungry.

**Chain used:** feel → metaphor (tabs) → small funny detail  
**Steal this:** tech metaphors are fine if they carry feeling, not jargon for show.

---

### Q5 — story / memory

**Q:** Tell me about one time you really enjoyed it.

**A (programmer default):**  
> Last weekend we played badminton. It was fun.

**A (sensory chat):**  
> Last weekend we played until it got dark. Someone kept apologizing for bad shots, and we all got worse on purpose just to make it fair — it was ridiculous. Walking home, the air felt cooler, and for once I wasn't replaying work messages in my head. Small evening, but it stayed with me.

**Chain used:** scene → one detail → feeling → soft landing  
**Steal this:** end with *"it stayed with me"* — emotional residue, not a plot summary.

---

### Q6 — door / preference contrast

**Q:** Team sports or solo?

**A (programmer default):**  
> Solo. More flexible.

**A (sensory chat):**  
> Both, for different moods. Solo when I need quiet — walk, clear the fog. With people when I want noise and bad jokes. Team stuff forces me out of my own head, which I secretly need. You?

**Chain used:** soft both → scene for each → honest need → door  
**Steal this:** *"which I secretly need"* = vulnerability without oversharing.

## Side-by-side: the upgrade pattern

| Layer | Programmer | Sensory chat |
|---|---|---|
| Stance | Yes / No | Kind of / depends / lately |
| Content | Fact list | One scene + one detail |
| Feeling | Missing or "good" | Body / mood / metaphor |
| Length | 1–5 words | 2–4 short sentences |
| End | Full stop | Door (question / invite) |

---

## Reusable sentence shells (sports & movement)

1. **"Kind of… not in a hardcore way."**  
   → Soft entry for any hobby you're mild about.

2. **"I'm not training for anything. I just ___."**  
   → Removes performance pressure; sounds human.

3. **"When work is heavy, I ___, and my body feels ___."**  
   → Links lifestyle + body sensation.

4. **"Tired in a clean way."**  
   → Better than "good tired."

5. **"Half of the tabs in my head finally close."**  
   → Feeling via metaphor.

6. **"Small evening, but it stayed with me."**  
   → Ends a story with emotional weight.

7. **"Both, for different moods."**  
   → Escapes false either/or.

8. **"Which I secretly need."**  
   → Soft honesty; good for connection.

## Grammar & naturalness notes

| Watch | Fix |
|---|---|
| ❌ Do you like **play** sports? | ✅ Do you like **playing** sports? / Do you like sports? |
| ❌ I very like | ✅ I **really** like / I kind of like |
| ❌ I am boring when I sit | ✅ I **get bored** / sitting all day **bores me** (*bored* = my feeling) |
| ❌ every times | ✅ **every time** / **sometimes** |
| Softeners help | kind of, sort of, a bit, honestly, lately |
