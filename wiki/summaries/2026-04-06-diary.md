## Topic
AI's role in learning — particularly language learning — and the importance of feedback loops.

## Key Ideas
1. AI technology has advanced dramatically and benefits ordinary people
2. AI enables learning things previously out of reach (e.g. English conversations)
3. Feedback is the most important factor in effective learning
4. Programming proved this: rapid verification of right/wrong accelerates skill building
5. With AI, the same rapid feedback loop is now available for language learning

## Vocabulary Extracted
dramatically, obsolete, invaluable, accelerate, acquisition, achievable, verify, resolve

## Phrases Learned
on the contrary, out of reach, feedback loop, no shortcut besides, open doors, build up

## Error Patterns Found
- Subject-verb agreement with does/do
- Overuse of "really/very" as intensifiers
- Comparative form errors (more quickly → quicker)
- Parallel structure in lists
- Missing paragraph breaks

## Writing Lessons
- One strong verb beats "very + adjective"
- Organize into 3 paragraphs: intro, body, conclusion
- End sentences/paragraphs with the most important word
