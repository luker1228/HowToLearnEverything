## Error Patterns Found

- Unnatural weather collocation ("favourite weather is rainy day") → more natural as "favourite kind of weather is rainy days".

## Writing Lessons
- Use sensory details (sound, smell, and sight) to describe weather.
- Make comparisons between different regions to explain experiences.
- Use simple, clear sentences instead of complicated structures.
- Small grammar fixes (tense, articles, collocations) can make your ideas sound much more natural.
