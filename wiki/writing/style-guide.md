# Personal Style Guide

Goals and notes on developing a personal English writing style.

---

## Current Level
- IELTS estimated: ~5.0-5.5
- Strengths: Active voice, logical flow, clear opinions
- Weaknesses: Typos, overused intensifiers, paragraph structure, comparatives

## Goals
- Write naturally and conversationally (not overly formal)
- Eliminate recurring grammar errors (subject-verb agreement, parallel structure)
- Build vocabulary beyond basic words (replace "very/really" with precise words)
- Develop proper paragraph structure instinctively

## Style Preferences
- Practical, conversational English over academic writing
- Focus on expressing ideas clearly and naturally
- Learn through diary writing and iterative correction

## Progress Markers
- [ ] Consistently use correct comparative forms
- [ ] No more "really/very" as default intensifiers
- [ ] Every diary entry has clear paragraph breaks
- [ ] Zero typos in common words (people, believed, English)
