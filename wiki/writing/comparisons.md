# Original vs Improved Comparisons

Side-by-side examples showing how corrections improve writing.

## Diary 2026-04-06

### Full Text Comparison

**Original:**
> Hello, everybody. Today, I want to talk about my new findings on learning. Recently, the technology of AI has developed a very big improvement. For common pepople, what can we get from AI? Does it means that you don't need to learn anything more? On the contrary, for me, I think AI gives us more time and resources to learn something that common people couldn't learn before. Such as dialog with someone with English, practice and correct your skills. I have always belived that feedback is the best way to do anything, especially in learning. The more quickly it is, the more deep you learn. I have mastered 4-5 kinds of programming language. There is no easier way but practicing more. Sometimes, you don't know your practice is effective. And you don't know whether you're right or wrong. But programming is a something you can make sure whether you're right or wrong quickly. So, I have mastered many skills. In the next AI generation, I really want to make my Enligh better or even other languages. With AI, I can correct my mistakes, and do more practice. It is really exciting.

**Improved:**
> Hello, everybody. Today, I want to share my new findings on learning. Recently, AI technology has improved dramatically. For ordinary people, what benefits can we gain from AI? Does this mean you no longer need to learn anything more? On the contrary, I think AI gives us more time and resources to learn things that were previously out of reach, such as holding conversations in English, practicing, and correcting your skills. I have always believed that feedback is the best approach to anything, especially in learning. The quicker the feedback, the deeper the learning.
>
> I have mastered 4-5 programming languages. There is no shortcut besides practicing more. Sometimes you wonder whether your practice is effective or whether you are right or wrong. But programming is something where you can verify correctness immediately. So I have built up many skills.
>
> In the AI era ahead, I want to improve my English and learn other languages. With AI, I can correct my mistakes and practice more. This is truly exciting.

### Key Changes

| # | Technique Applied | What Changed |
|---|-------------------|--------------|
| 1 | Omit needless words | "the technology of AI has developed a very big improvement" -> "AI technology has improved dramatically" |
| 2 | Use specific language | "common people" -> "ordinary people" |
| 3 | Fix grammar | "Does it means" -> "Does this mean" |
| 4 | Parallel structure | "practice and correct" -> "practicing and correcting" |
| 5 | Better comparatives | "more quickly...more deep" -> "quicker...deeper" |
| 6 | Paragraph breaks | One block -> 3 focused paragraphs |
| 7 | Precise verbs | "make my English better" -> "improve my English" |

## Laughter Example

### Sentence Comparison

**Original:**
> So, it means that laughing like flu, even you do a good handwashing, you can't protect yourself against it.

**Improved:**
> So it means that laughter is like the flu: even if you wash your hands well, you still can't protect yourself from it.

### Key Changes

| # | Technique Applied | What Changed |
|---|-------------------|--------------|
| 1 | Natural nouns | "laughing" -> "laughter" (use the noun to talk about the general idea) |
| 2 | Add article | "flu" -> "the flu" (natural in this comparison) |
| 3 | Correct conditional | "even you do a good handwashing" -> "even if you wash your hands well" |
| 4 | Natural collocation | "a good handwashing" -> "wash your hands" |
| 5 | Preposition choice | "protect yourself against it" -> "protect yourself from it" (both possible, but "from" sounds more natural here) |

## Rainy Days Description (2026-04-08)

### Paragraph Comparison

**Original:**
> Rainy days always give you a feeling of peace. Firstly, you are out rainy day to watch it instead of in the rainy day. Being wet is not comfortable and may catch cold. When it rains, the white noise is really comfortable. Especailly you're in a cozy and warm room. You'll feel very safe and peace. What's more , the air will be very fresh. And many animals will come out , like frogs and so on.

**Improved:**
> "Rainy days always give me a sense of peace. First, it’s best to watch the rain from indoors rather than being outside in it — getting wet isn’t comfortable, and you could catch a cold. The sound of rain creates a soothing white noise, especially when you’re inside a cozy, warm room. It makes you feel safe and peaceful. What’s more, the air feels really fresh after the rain, and you can see animals like frogs come out."

### Key Changes

| # | Technique Applied | What Changed |
|---|-------------------|--------------|
| 1 | Pronoun choice | "give you a feeling of peace" -> "give me a sense of peace" (more personal and natural) |
| 2 | Clarity & structure | Replaced confusing sentence about "out rainy day" with a clear contrast between being indoors vs outdoors in the rain. |
| 3 | Natural collocations | "white noise is really comfortable" -> "creates a soothing white noise"; "safe and peace" -> "safe and peaceful". |
| 4 | Word choice | "may catch cold" -> "could catch a cold"; "will be very fresh" -> "feels really fresh". |
