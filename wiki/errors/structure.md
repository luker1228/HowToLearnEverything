# Sentence & Paragraph Structure

Patterns to improve at the sentence and paragraph level.

---

## Paragraph Organization

**Issue:** Writing everything in one large block of text.

**Fix:** Split into clear paragraphs, each with one main idea:
1. Introduction — state your topic and position
2. Body — develop with examples and evidence
3. Conclusion — connect to personal goals, wrap up

**Source:** diary 2026-04-06 was one continuous paragraph; restructured into 3.

---

## Wordiness

**Issue:** Using more words than necessary.

| Wordy | Concise |
|-------|---------|
| "the technology of AI has developed a very big improvement" | "AI technology has improved dramatically" |
| "make sure whether you're right or wrong quickly" | "verify correctness immediately" |
| "In my opinion, I think that..." | "I believe..." |
| "4-5 kinds of programming language" | "4-5 programming languages" |

**Rule:** If a sentence says the same thing after cutting words, cut them.

---

## Positive Form

**Issue:** Stating things in negative form when positive is clearer.

| Negative | Positive |
|----------|----------|
| "you don't know whether your practice is effective" | "you wonder whether your practice is effective" |

**Rule:** Put statements in positive form. Use "not" for denial/antithesis, not as evasion.
