# Word Choice Patterns

Common word choice issues and better alternatives.

---

## Overused Intensifiers

| Overused | Better alternatives | Note |
|----------|-------------------|------|
| "really" | truly, genuinely, significantly | Appeared 3 times in one diary entry. Weakens writing when overused. |
| "very big" | dramatic, significant, substantial | "Very + adjective" is almost always weaker than one precise word. |

**Rule of thumb:** If you're reaching for "really" or "very", find a stronger single word instead.

---

## Vague vs. Specific

| Vague | Specific | Why |
|-------|----------|-----|
| "common people" | "ordinary people" | Standard collocation |
| "get from AI" | "gain from AI" / "benefit from AI" | More precise verb |
| "do anything" | "approach anything" | More specific action |
| "has developed a very big improvement" | "has improved dramatically" | One precise verb beats a wordy noun phrase |
| "laughing like flu" | "laughter is like the flu" | Use the noun "laughter" and add "the" before "flu" for a natural comparison. |
| "my favourite weather is rainy day" | "my favourite kind of weather is rainy days" | Uses the natural collocation "kind of weather" and plural "rainy days". |
