# Grammar Error Patterns

Recurring grammar mistakes identified from writing practice.

---

## Subject-Verb Agreement

| Error | Correction | Rule |
|-------|-----------|------|
| "Does it **means**..." | "Does it **mean**..." | After "does", use base form of verb (no -s) |

**Frequency:** Common with question forms using do/does/did.

---

## Articles

| Error | Correction | Rule |
|-------|-----------|------|
| "programming is **a** something" | "programming is something" | "Something" is an indefinite pronoun; never takes an article |

**Frequency:** Occasional overuse of articles before pronouns.

---

## Comparative Structures

| Error | Correction | Rule |
|-------|-----------|------|
| "the more quickly..., the more deep..." | "the quicker..., the deeper..." | Use "the + comparative + ..., the + comparative + ..." pattern; one-syllable adjectives take -er, not "more" |

**Frequency:** First occurrence. Watch for irregular comparatives.

---

## Parallel Structure

| Error | Correction | Rule |
|-------|-----------|------|
| "practice and correct your skills" | "practicing and correcting your skills" | Items in a list must share the same grammatical form |

**Frequency:** Common in lists and compound verb phrases.

---

## Pluralization

| Error | Correction | Rule |
|-------|-----------|------|
| "4-5 kinds of programming language" | "4-5 programming languages" | Countable nouns after numbers must be plural |

**Frequency:** Occasional.

---

## Conditionals ("even if")

| Error | Correction | Rule |
|-------|-----------|------|
| "even you do a good handwashing" | "even if you wash your hands well" | Use "even if" to introduce a condition and a verb phrase ("wash your hands") instead of a noun phrase ("a handwashing"). |

**Frequency:** First occurrence. Watch for conditional clauses starting with "even".

---

## Verb Tenses (past vs present)

| Error | Correction | Rule |
|-------|-----------|------|
| "During my childhood, I live in the north of China..." | "During my childhood, I lived in a very small city in Inner Mongolia, in northern China." | Use past simple ("lived") for completed periods in the past, especially with time phrases like "during my childhood". |

**Frequency:** First occurrence. Watch for mixing present tense with clear past-time expressions.

---

## Noun Phrases (a/an)

| Error | Correction | Rule |
|-------|-----------|------|
| "My logic is really mess." | "My logic is really a mess." | When the complement is a singular countable noun ("mess"), use an article ("a"). |

**Frequency:** First occurrence. Watch for missing articles before singular countable nouns.
