# Laughter and the Flu Metaphor

This short passage compares **laughter** to the **flu**. It says:

> but unlike the flu, even a good handwashing will not protect you against breaking into a fit of laughter.

The idea is that laughter is described as if it were **contagious**, like a **virus**. However, no amount of careful **handwashing** or good **hygiene** can prevent you from suddenly **bursting into a fit of laughter** when something is truly funny.

This text is used to practice understanding metaphors and natural expressions like "burst into laughter" and "a fit of laughter".
