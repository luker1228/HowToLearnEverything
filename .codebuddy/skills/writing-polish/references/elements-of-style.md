# The Elements of Style - Key Principles

Based on *The Elements of Style* by Strunk & White (1918)

## The Core Rules

### 1. Make the paragraph the unit of composition
- One central idea per paragraph
- Develop ideas with specific details and examples
- Paragraphs should flow logically into each other

### 2. Use active voice
Passive voice is weaker and often ambiguous.

| Passive (avoid) | Active (prefer) |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| "The experiment was conducted by me" | "I conducted the experiment" |
| "Improvements can be seen in the data" | "The data shows improvements" |

### 3. Put statements in positive form
Negative constructions add complexity without clarity.

| Negative (avoid) | Positive (prefer) |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| "not unkind" | "kind" |
| "do not miss" | "attend" |

### 4. Use definite, specific, concrete language
Vague language weakens arguments.

| Vague | Specific |
|-------|----------|
| "things" | "policies", "measures" |
| "a lot of people" | "millions of workers" |

### 5. Omit needless words (THE most important rule)
Every word should earn its place.

| Wordy | Concise |
|-------|---------|
| "due to the fact that" | "because" |
| "have a tendency to" | "tend to" |
| "it is necessary that" | "must" |

### 6. Avoid superfluous sentences
If a sentence doesn't advance your argument, delete it.

### 7. Use parallel structure
Items in a series must have the same grammatical form.

| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| "running, swimming, and to bike" | "running, swimming, and biking" |

### 8. Keep related words together
Misplaced modifiers create confusion.

### 9. Use transitional expressions sparingly
Let the logic of sentences show relationships without explicit transitions.

### 10. Put the emphatic word at the end
The last word of a sentence receives the most emphasis.

## Common Issues for Chinese Learners

### Direct Translation Patterns

| Literal (wrong) | Natural English |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| "I very like" | "I really like" |
| "eat medicine" | "take medicine" |

### Overused Intensifiers

Remove: **very, really, actually, quite, pretty, so**

| Wordy | Concise |
|-------|---------|
| "very important" | "essential" or "key" |
| "really think" | "think" |

### Comparative Structure

"The + comparative..., the + comparative..."

| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| "The more quickly..., the more deep..." | "The quicker..., the deeper..." |
| "more better" | "better" |
