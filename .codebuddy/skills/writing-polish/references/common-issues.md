# Common IELTS Writing Issues

Patterns to watch for in IELTS essays from Chinese English learners.

## Vague Language

| Vague | Specific |
|-------|----------|
| "some negative effects" | "negative effects on sleep patterns" |
| "a lot of money" | "thousands of dollars" |
| "many people" | "millions of workers" |
| "things" | Never use in academic writing |

## Wordiness

| Wordy | Concise |
|-------|---------|
| "In this essay, I will discuss..." | Remove entirely |
| "First of all" | "First," or just start |
| "due to the fact that" | "because" |

## Overused Intensifiers

Remove: **very, really, actually, quite, pretty, so**

## Common Collocation Errors

| Incorrect | Correct |
|-----------|---------|
| "fast speed" | "high speed" |
| "open lights" | "turn on the lights" |
| "eat medicine" | "take medicine" |
| "discuss about" | "discuss" |

## Article Errors

| Incorrect | Correct |
|-----------|---------|
| "the society" (general) | "society" |
| "the future" (general) | "future" |

## Sentence Structure

### Parallel Structure
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| "running, swimming, and to bike" | "running, swimming, and biking" |

### Subject-Verb Agreement
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| "This make people feel" | "This makes people feel" |

## Transitional Expressions

Use sparingly. Better to let logic flow naturally.

Overused:
- "Firstly" / "Secondly" / "Finally"
- "However" - use once per essay
- "Moreover" / "Furthermore"

## Direct Translations from Chinese

| Literal (wrong) | Natural English |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| "I very like it" | "I really like it" |
