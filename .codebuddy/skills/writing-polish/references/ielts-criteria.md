# IELTS Writing Criteria Explained

## Band Score Descriptors

### Task Achievement (25%)
- Fully addresses all parts of the task
- Presents a well-developed response with relevant, extended ideas
- Takes a clear position (not fence-sitting)
- Develops ideas with reasons, examples, and evidence

### Coherence and Cohesion (25%)
- Logical organization of ideas
- Clear progression throughout
- Appropriate use of cohesive devices (but not overuse)
- Proper paragraphing that aids readability

**Common mistakes:**
- Overusing transitions
- Paragraphs without clear topic sentences
- Illogical flow between ideas

### Lexical Resource (25%)
- Wide range of vocabulary
- Appropriate precision for context
- Correct word formation
- Correct collocations
- Formal register for academic writing

**Common issues:**
- Word form errors
- Collocation errors
- Overuse of "very", "really", "important"

### Grammatical Range and Accuracy (25%)
- Wide range of sentence structures
- Correct use of simple, compound, and complex sentences
- Accurate grammar and punctuation

**Key structures to demonstrate:**
- Simple sentences for clarity
- Compound sentences with coordinating conjunctions
- Complex sentences with subordinate clauses
- Relative clauses
- Conditionals (if/unless)

**Common grammar errors:**
- Subject-verb agreement
- Article usage (a, an, the)
- Preposition errors
- Tense inconsistency

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] Answering all parts of the question?
- [ ] Clear position stated?
- [ ] Ideas developed with examples?
- [ ] Paragraphs well-organized?
- [ ] Transitions used naturally, not overused?
- [ ] Vocabulary precise and varied?
- [ ] Word forms correct?
- [ ] Collocations natural?
- [ ] Sentence structures varied?
- [ ] Grammar accurate?
- [ ] No spelling errors?
- [ ] Formal register maintained?
