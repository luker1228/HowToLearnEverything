In this essay, I will discuss about whether technology has changed the way we live. I think that technology has changed our lives very much in both positive and negative ways.

Firstly, technology has made our life more convenience. We can order food and shop online without going out. This is very useful for busy people. Also, we can communicate with friends and family through social media and video calls. This make people feel more connect to each other.

However, there are also some negative effects. Many people spend too much time on their phones and computers. This can cause health problems like eye strain and back pain. Also, some people become addicted to video games and social media. This is very serious problem.

In conclusion, although technology has bring us many benefits, we should also be aware of its disadvantages. I believe that the positive aspects outweigh the negative ones if we use it responsibly.
