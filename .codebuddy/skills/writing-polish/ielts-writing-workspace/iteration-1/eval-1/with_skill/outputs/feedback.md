## Elements of Style Issues

### 1. Omit Needless Words (Most Important Rule)
- **"In this essay, I will discuss about"** → "I will discuss" (remove "about" and "In this essay")
- **"in both positive and negative ways"** → "in positive and negative ways"

### 2. Use Active Voice
- The essay generally uses active voice correctly, which is good.
- **Issue in Para 3:** "Many people spend too much time" is correctly active, but consider: "Excessive screen time causes health problems" is more direct and emphatic.

### 3. Put Statements in Positive Form
- No significant negative constructions to transform. Good.

### 4. Use Definite, Specific, Concrete Language
- **"very useful"** → specify *why* it's useful or what specific technology enables this
- **"health problems like eye strain and back pain"** → good specific examples, keep this approach
- **"some negative effects"** → be specific about which effects

### 5. Make the Paragraph the Unit of Composition
- **Paragraph 2** has only 2 sentences. Expand with a specific example of how technology has made life more convenient.
- **Paragraph 3** also needs development. Consider separating health problems and addiction into distinct supporting points.

### 6. Avoid Superfluous Sentences
- No truly superfluous sentences, but some sentences could be combined:
  - "This is very useful for busy people" could be integrated: "This is particularly useful for busy people who lack time."

### 7. Use Parallel Structure
- **"positive and negative ways"** is acceptable, but "positive and negative aspects" or "benefits and drawbacks" would be more parallel.

### 8. Keep Related Words Together
- **"very much in both positive and negative ways"** — "very much" is separated from what it could modify. Restructure.

### 9. Use Transitional Expressions Sparingly
- Only one transition used ("However"). This is fine—better than overuse.

### 10. Put the Emphatic Word at the End
- In the conclusion, "if we use it responsibly" is appropriately emphatic. Good.

## Overall Suggestions

### Top 5 Priority Improvements

1. **Eliminate grammar errors** (subject-verb agreement, word forms, articles). These are the biggest barriers to a higher band score.

2. **Develop paragraphs with specific examples.** Each paragraph should have 3-4 sentences with concrete supporting details, not just general statements.

3. **Replace vague language with specific vocabulary.** Instead of "very useful," explain *what* is useful and *for whom*.

4. **Use more sophisticated linking.** Replace "Also" with "Additionally," "Furthermore," or integrate ideas with subordinating conjunctions like "because," "although," "which."

5. **Vary sentence structure.** Incorporate complex sentences with relative clauses and conditionals, especially in the conclusion.

## Sample Essay

**Topic:** Technology has changed the way we live. Do you agree or disagree?

---

In an increasingly connected world, technology has profoundly transformed nearly every aspect of daily life. While this shift has brought undeniable benefits, it has also introduced certain challenges that deserve careful consideration.

The most significant advantage of technological progress lies in its ability to simplify tasks that once consumed considerable time and effort. Online services now allow people to order groceries, access medical consultations, and conduct banking transactions without leaving home. For professionals with demanding schedules, this convenience is particularly valuable. Furthermore, communication tools such as video calling and instant messaging havebridged geographical distances, enabling families and friends to maintain relationships across continents.

However, the ubiquity of technology is not without its drawbacks. Excessive screen time has been linked to various health concerns, including vision problems, musculoskeletal issues, and disrupted sleep patterns. Equally concerning is the growing prevalence of digital addiction, particularly among young people who may develop compulsive habits around gaming or social media. These behaviors can interfere with academic performance, social development, and mental well-being.

Despite these concerns, I believe that the benefits of technology outweigh its disadvantages when used mindfully. The key lies in establishing healthy boundaries—setting time limits, prioritizing face-to-face interactions, and using technology as a tool rather than a substitute for real-world experiences. With such an approach, individuals can harness the power of innovation while mitigating its risks.

In conclusion, technology has indeed transformed modern life, offering remarkable convenience and connectivity. While its negative effects warrant attention, responsible usage can maximize its advantages for future generations.

---

### What Makes This Essay Effective

1. **Clear thesis statement** in the first sentence, establishing the essay's direction immediately.

2. **Specific examples** in paragraph 2 (online groceries, medical consultations, banking) rather than vague generalizations.

3. **Topic sentences** at the beginning of each body paragraph clearly signal what the paragraph will cover.

4. **Balanced treatment** of both sides before the writer's position in paragraph 4.

5. **Complex sentence structures** demonstrate grammatical range (e.g., "While this shift has brought undeniable benefits, it has also introduced certain challenges").

6. **Sophisticated vocabulary** used appropriately: "profoundly," "ubiquity," "prevalence," "mitigating."

7. **Strong conclusion** that synthesizes the argument and ends with an forward-looking statement.

8. **Transitional devices** used effectively but not excessively: "Furthermore," "However," "Equally concerning," "Despite these concerns," "In conclusion."
