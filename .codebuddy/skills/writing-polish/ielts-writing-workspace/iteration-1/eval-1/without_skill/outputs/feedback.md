# IELTS Essay Feedback: Technology and Its Impact on Our Lives

## Original Essay

In this essay, I will discuss about whether technology has changed the way we live. I think that technology has changed our lives very much in both positive and negative ways.

Firstly, technology has made our life more convenience. We can order food and shop online without going out. This is very useful for busy people. Also, we can communicate with friends and family through social media and video calls. This make people feel more connect to each other.

However, there are also some negative effects. Many people spend too much time on their phones and computers. This can cause health problems like eye strain and back pain. Also, some people become addicted to video games and social media. This is very serious problem.

In conclusion, although technology has bring us many benefits, we should also be aware of its disadvantages. I believe that the positive aspects outweigh the negative ones if we use it responsibly.

---

## Detailed Feedback Based on The Elements of Style

### 1. Rule of Clarity: Use definite, specific, concrete language

**Issue:** The essay uses vague language throughout.

- "changed our lives very much" is imprecise. What specifically has changed? How much?

**Suggestion:** Replace vague phrases with specific examples:
- Instead of: "technology has changed our lives very much"
- Try: "technology has transformed how we communicate, shop, and access information"

---

### 2. Principle of Unity: One sentence should not begin with the conjunction "And" or "But"

**Issue:** The essay uses "Also" to connect sentences, which creates a list-like structure rather than flowing paragraphs.

**Suggestion:** Use transition words more sparingly. Each paragraph should develop a single idea with sentences that naturally follow from each other.

---

### 3. Voice and Verb Tense: Use active voice

**Issue:** The essay mostly uses active voice correctly, but some sentences are awkward.

- "This make people feel more connect" — subject-verb disagreement and wrong verb form

**Suggestion:** "This makes people feel more connected to each other."

---

### 4. Wordiness: Omit needless words

**Issue:** Several sentences are wordy or redundant.

- "I think that technology has changed our lives very much in both positive and negative ways."
- "This is very serious problem." (missing article)

**Suggestions:**
- "I believe technology has significantly changed our lives."
- "This is a very serious problem." or "This is a serious problem."

---

### 5. Sentence Structure: Express coordinate ideas in similar form

**Issue:** The essay mixes sentence structures without parallel construction.

- "Firstly, technology has made our life more convenience. We can order food..."
- Lists like "eye strain and back pain" are fine, but transitions between ideas are abrupt.

**Suggestion:** Maintain consistent sentence openings within paragraphs.

---

### 6. Specific Grammar and Word Choice Issues

| Location | Issue | Correction |
|----------|-------|------------|
| Line 1 | "discuss about" | "discuss" (not "discuss about") |
| Line 1 | "changed our lives very much" | "significantly changed our lives" |
| Line 3 | "life more convenience" | "lives more convenient" |

| Line 5 | "bring us many benefits" | "brought us many benefits" (past tense in conclusion) |
| Line 5 | "very serious problem" | "a serious problem" (missing article) |

---

### 7. Paragraph Structure

**Current Structure:**
- Introduction: States the topic
- Paragraph 2: Positive effects (mostly good)
- Paragraph 3: Negative effects (mostly good)
- Conclusion: Summarizes (too brief)

**Issues:**
- Introduction is weak — avoid "In this essay, I will discuss about"
- Conclusion is underdeveloped

**Suggestions:**
- Introduction: Get to the point quickly. "Technology has fundamentally transformed modern life, bringing both significant benefits and notable drawbacks."
- Conclusion: Expand to restate the thesis more elegantly and offer a final thought.

---

### 8. Cohesion and Flow

The essay lists ideas but doesn't connect them well. Transitions like "Firstly," "However," and "Also" are mechanical.

**Better approach:**
- Positive paragraph: Present the benefits with specific examples
- Negative paragraph: Present drawbacks with cause-effect reasoning
- Conclusion: Weigh the arguments and offer insight

## Revised Sample Essay

**Technology and Its Impact on Modern Life**

Technology has fundamentally transformed how we live, work, and communicate, bringing both significant benefits and notable drawbacks.

On the positive side, technology has made daily life far more convenient. We can order food, shop, and access services online without leaving home, which saves precious time for busy professionals. Furthermore, social media and video calls allow us to maintain relationships with friends and family across vast distances, making the world feel more connected than ever before.

However, these advantages come with serious downsides. Excessive screen time leads to health issues such as eye strain, back pain, and disrupted sleep patterns. Moreover, addiction to video games and social media has become a growing concern, particularly among young people, affecting their academic performance and social development.

In conclusion, while technology has undoubtedly enriched our lives in many ways, we must remain mindful of its potential harms. By using technology responsibly and setting healthy boundaries, we can maximize its benefits while minimizing its drawbacks.
